Privacy Policy for Trace

Last updated: 17 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Trace ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is published by sainilabs. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Trace is a single-player arcade game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or files. The App works fully offline except for serving ads.

Information collected

On your device only (never sent to us): Your best score, best distance, run count, sound setting, and remove-ads purchase status are stored locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob): The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and similar identifiers.
  • General, coarse information about your device and app usage needed to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising and consent

Ads are shown only after a run ends — never during gameplay. In regions where it is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the App presents a consent request, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, before any personalized ads are served. If you decline, you will still see non-personalized ads.

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchases

The App offers a single optional one-time purchase to remove interstitial ads. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment details. See Google Play's terms and privacy policy for how that data is handled.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads, please supervise use by minors.

Data security and retention

The only data the App itself stores is the on-device game progress described above. It is retained until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads: your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Remove all locally stored game data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
