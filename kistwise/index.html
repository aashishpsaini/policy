Privacy Policy for Kistwise

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile app Kistwise: EMI & Loan Planner ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is published by sainilabs. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Kistwise is a set of loan, affordability, tax and savings calculators with an optional loan tracker and expense tracker. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, SMS, photos, location, microphone or files. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you turn on family sharing. The calculators work offline; the App uses the network only to show ads and to download the current lender and government rates once a day.

Information you enter, stored on your device only

The App stores, in its private storage on your phone: the loans you add (amount, rate, tenure, dates, prepayments and rate changes), the expenses and income you log, budgets, bills, goals and assets, your saved calculations and last-used inputs, an optional first name and monthly take-home pay that you choose to enter, and your settings. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

If you attach receipt photos, the App copies the photos you pick (through Android's photo picker) into its private storage. If you import a bank or card statement, the file you pick is read on your phone and only the transactions you choose are saved; the file itself is not kept or sent anywhere.

You can turn on encryption in Settings, which locks the data file with a key kept in your phone's secure keystore.

Android's standard backup may include the App's data in your own Google account backup if you have device backup turned on (encrypted data can't be restored on a different phone). You can also save a backup file yourself, or turn on auto backup, which writes a copy to a file location you pick, such as Google Drive. Where that file goes, and that provider's handling of it, is your choice.

Spotting payments from notifications (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Spot payments automatically" and give the App notification access in Android settings, the App looks at new notifications from a fixed list of UPI and bank apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and major banks' apps) to suggest expenses and income for you to add. Android delivers every notification to an app with this access, but the App ignores all of them unless the feature is on and the notification comes from one of those payment apps. It never looks at SMS, chat, email or any other app's notifications.

From a payment notification the App keeps only the amount, whether money went out or came in, the payee or payer name, the app's name, the time and a suggested category, in its private storage on your phone. The notification text itself is not saved, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. A spotted payment is only added to your expenses when you tap Add. You can turn the feature off in the App at any time, which stops it straight away, and remove notification access in Android settings.

Family sharing (optional, off by default)

If you set up family sharing, you pick a folder through Android's system folder picker, for example one kept in sync by a cloud storage app you and your family use. The App then writes a small summary file to that folder and reads the summaries other family members' phones have written there. The summary holds only what you choose to share: the name you enter for yourself, monthly spending totals by category, budgets, bills (name, amount, due day, paid months) and goals, and, only if you turn it on, loan totals (number of loans, total outstanding, total monthly EMI, debt-free date) with no lender names, loan names or rates. Individual transactions and their notes are never written. The file goes only to the folder you picked; we never receive it, and how it is stored and synced is up to the provider of that folder. Leaving family sharing deletes this phone's summary file from the folder.

Current rates

To keep the repo rate, small-savings rates and the lender rate table current, the App downloads a public file (https://aashishpsaini.github.io/kistwise/rates.json) at most once a day. The request carries none of your data; like any web request, GitHub, which hosts the file, sees your IP address and the time. If the download fails, the App uses the rates it already has.

Kistwise Pro purchases

Kistwise Pro is sold as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; the App only learns whether your Google account owns Pro (and, for the subscription, whether it renews) and never sees your payment details. You can cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Ratings, invites and shared links

The App may occasionally show Google Play's own rating card; your rating goes to Google Play, not to us. Invite messages and shared calculations are sent only when you choose to share them, through the app you pick. A shared calculation is a link to the matching page on our website with the numbers from that calculation in the link itself, so anyone you send it to can see those numbers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a small banner ad below calculator results. There are no full-screen ads, and no ads at all on My loans or Money, where your personal numbers are shown. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect a device advertising identifier and general information about your device and app usage. They never receive the loans, expenses or other figures you enter. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the App asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. If you decline, you see non-personalised ads. You can change this from Settings → Ad privacy choices, where offered, and reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Notifications and app lock

If you turn on reminders, the App posts local notifications before your EMIs and bills are due. They are scheduled on your phone and never go through a server. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock; the App never sees your biometric data or PIN.

Not financial advice

Results are illustrations based on the inputs and assumptions shown. They are not financial, tax, investment or insurance advice. The App does not offer loans, payments, trading or any other financial service, and does not connect to your bank.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: delete items in the App, or clear its storage, or uninstall it.
  • Family sharing: leave the family in the App to remove your summary file, or change what you share at any time.
  • Payment spotting: turn it off in the App, and remove notification access in Android settings.
  • Ad consent: Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App, where offered.
  • Advertising ID: your device's Google → Ads settings.
