Privacy Policy for Merge Meadow

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Merge Meadow: Garden Makeover ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is published by sainilabs. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Merge Meadow is a single-player merge and restoration game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or files. There is no sign-in and no analytics. The game is fully playable offline; an internet connection is only used to serve ads and process optional purchases.

Information collected

On your device only (never sent to us): your garden and town progress, level, coins and energy, story progress and friendships, daily gift streak, daily missions and spins, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders) are stored locally using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob): The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and similar identifiers.
  • General, coarse information about your device and app usage needed to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising and consent

Most ads in Merge Meadow are optional videos you choose to watch for a bonus, such as extra energy, doubled daily gifts, an extra spin, or popping a bonus bubble. Declining always costs you nothing. Occasionally a full-screen ad may appear at a natural break (after restoring an area or leaving the board), or when you return to the App after some time away. Ads never interrupt a merge.

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the App presents a consent request, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, before any personalized ads are served. If you decline, you will still see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App.

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your energy is full or your daily gift is ready). They are created by the App itself; no notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

In-app purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to remove full-screen ads (optional reward videos remain available). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment details.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads, please supervise use by minors.

Data security and retention

The only data the App itself stores is the on-device game progress and preferences described above. It is retained until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy options in the App.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads: your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Turn off reminders: Settings → Daily reminders in the App.
  • Remove all locally stored game data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
