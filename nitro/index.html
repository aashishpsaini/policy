Privacy Policy for NITRO

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game NITRO: Highway Racing & Cops ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is published by sainilabs. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

NITRO is a single-player arcade racing game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or files. There is no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own. The game is fully playable offline; an internet connection is only used to serve ads and process optional purchases.

Information collected

On your device only (never sent to us): your cash, cars, upgrades and paint choices, rival ladder progress, best distances, daily-bonus streak, settings (music and effects volume, vibration, steering, units, graphics) and the identifiers of purchases you have already received (so a purchase is never granted twice) are stored locally using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

Motion sensor: if you choose tilt steering, the App reads your device's accelerometer in real time to steer the car. These readings are used instantly on the device, are not stored, and are never transmitted. Vibration: the App triggers your device's vibration motor for game feedback; no data is collected.

Collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob): The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and similar identifiers.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived.
  • General information about your device and how you interact with the ads and App (such as app launches, taps and video views) and diagnostic information needed to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising and consent

Most ads in NITRO are optional videos you choose to watch for a benefit: continuing a run after a crash, doubling the cash from a race, doubling the daily bonus, or claiming free cash. Declining always costs you nothing. Occasionally a full-screen ad may appear between races (never during one, and not in your first few races). A one-time purchase removes these full-screen ads.

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App presents a consent request, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices inside the App (shown where applicable).

You can also reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchases

The App offers optional one-time purchases: removing full-screen ads, a starter pack, and packs of in-game cash. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment details. Google shares a purchase record (product and order identifiers) with the App so it can deliver what you bought, and the App can restore your ad-free purchase from Google Play on a new device.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads and contains purchases, please supervise use by minors.

Data security and retention

The only data the App itself stores is the on-device game progress and preferences described above. It is retained until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies. Data sent by the App to Google's advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy choices in the App.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads: your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Delete all locally stored game data: Settings → Reset in the App (progress only), or clear the App's storage / uninstall it.
  • Access, correct or delete data held by Google for advertising (for example under GDPR or the CCPA): use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. We hold no identifiable personal data about you, so there is nothing further for us to access or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy
