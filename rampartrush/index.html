Privacy Policy for Rampart Rush

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Rampart Rush ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is published by sainilabs. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Rampart Rush is a single-player game: you lead an army up a castle road, grow it at the gates and fight your way to a boss. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, microphone or files. The game itself works offline; it uses the network only to serve ads and for the optional purchase.

Information collected

On your device only (never sent to us): the level you have reached, your coins and chest keys, the stars and the furthest point reached on each level, the heroes you own and which one leads, your upgrade levels, the allies you have freed, their levels and which one marches with you, your win streak and best combo, the days of your daily reward calendar and daily battle, which tips have been shown, your sound, music, haptics and screen-shake settings, when the free-coins video is next offered, and whether you own Remove Ads. These are stored locally using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob): The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and similar identifiers.
  • General, coarse information about your device and app usage needed to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising and consent

Full-screen ads are shown only between battles, never during one, and not before level 5 or in your first battles of a session. There are no banner ads and no ads when you open the App. Several ads are optional and rewarded: you choose to watch them in exchange for a second wind after a defeat, doubled coins after a win, a doubled daily reward, or free coins in Upgrades, and declining costs you nothing.

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the App presents a consent request, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, before any ad is requested. If you decline personalized ads, you will still see non-personalized ads. You can reopen these choices any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the App, where it is offered.

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchases

The App offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads, which ends the full-screen ads between battles. The optional rewarded videos stay available. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment details. The App asks Google Play whether you own Remove Ads and records only that answer on your device. See Google Play's terms and privacy policy for how payment data is handled.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and is not enrolled in the "Designed for Families" programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads, please supervise use by minors.

Data security and retention

The only data the App itself stores is the on-device game progress described above. It is retained until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads: your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Change your ad-consent choices: the App's Settings → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset your progress: the App's Settings → Reset progress. This does not remove a purchase.
  • Remove all locally stored game data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy
